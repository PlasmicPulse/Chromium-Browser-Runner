---
name: Pet parking behavior
description: Keep the visitor's choice to stop roaming separate from animal care and preserve movable-bed edge access.
---

Parking the pet is a visitor preference, separate from the pet's care/rest state. Feeding, playing and cleaning must not automatically release a parked pet; explicit release or dragging the pet out does.

**Why:** The user requested a bed specifically so visitors can stop the pet wandering without losing access to the companion.

**How to apply:** Preserve parking across care actions and reloads. Keep autonomous wandering away from browser controls, but use the visible viewport rather than wandering safety insets for user dragging and docking, so a bed moved near an edge remains reachable and holds the pet correctly.
