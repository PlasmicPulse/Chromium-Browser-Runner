---
name: Local music storage
description: Product constraint for the music library's accepted sources and persistence.
---

Accept uploaded MP3 files only. Keep tracks in the visitor's current browser, with no YouTube/streaming links, cloud uploads, or cross-device sync.

**Why:** the user explicitly requested browser-local MP3 uploads only.

**How to apply:** preserve local-only storage when extending the music player; do not add remote sources or sync without asking.
